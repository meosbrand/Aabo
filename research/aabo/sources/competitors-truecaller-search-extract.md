## SMS filtering
- "Truecaller uses the same powerful algorithms used to identify spam callers in SMS as well."
- Smart SMS uses "state-of-the-art machine learning models that adapt based on the feedback you give it".
- SMS logic "is built into the app itself and it can work offline – nothing leaves your device, including all OTPs, bank SMSes and financial information."
- Suspicious message → red notification that persists until dismissed; opening the flagged text auto-disables its links; full access requires marking the sender as safe.
- Message ID: "None of your messages leave your device and everything is processed locally"; uses LLMs for "on-device scanning of the SMS inbox to identify important business messages".

## WhatsApp
- May 2023: caller identification extended to WhatsApp and other messaging apps, in beta, global rollout planned later that month.
- Prompted by a reported rise in WhatsApp spam-call reports in India within a two-week span.
- Android setting under Calls: option to identify numbers in other apps (WhatsApp, Telegram).
- Detection relies on a shared database of known spam numbers fed by user reports.
- User counts conflict: ~350 million (Reuters-based, India 250 million) vs 500 million (LinkedIn repost).
- Unverified Slashdot comment alleges the caller-ID database is built from users' address books — a privacy concern.
