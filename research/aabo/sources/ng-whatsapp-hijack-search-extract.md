# WhatsApp account hijacking in Nigeria (search extract)

Sources: https://dubawa.org/how-whatsapp-scammers-circumvent-security-features-to-swindle-nigerians/ , https://techpoint.africa/2019/09/13/whatsapp-account-hack/ ,
https://techeconomy.ng/nitda-issues-advisory-on-safeguarding-whatsapp-accounts , https://www.itedgenews.africa/nitda-to-nigerians-use-your-whatsapp-with-caution-data-thieves-are-on-the-prowl/ ,

- Dubawa: "Once they have the OTP, the hackers hijack the WhatsApp accounts and ask contacts for money." Victims called and told to click a link and read out the code; "The calm, reassuring voice behind the call instructed him."
- Techpoint 2019: hijacked accounts pushed an investment scheme into groups — "Several people calling to ask if you were responsible for sending messages about investing in a scheme on a WhatsApp group."
- Newer variant (vendor blog): fraudulent web interfaces trigger a legitimate device-pairing flow.
- NITDA Dec 2023: "Attackers often use social engineering tactics, such as calls or convincing text messages, to get users to reveal the activation code." Defence: "Go to WhatsApp Settings > Account > Two-Step Verification. Enable two-step verification and set up a secure PIN."
- NITDA Nov 2024 recovery: log out of all devices; "uninstall and reinstall WhatsApp" to request a new code; enable two-step verification immediately. Groups: remove the hijacked account, warn members about suspicious links, contact the victim via another app, report to WhatsApp.
- NCC-CSIRT (Apr 2023): "WhatsApp is increasingly becoming a prime target for hackers and scammers."
- NCC-CSIRT advisory dated 18 Aug 2026: phishing page promising "10GB of free mobile data and ₦5,000 airtime in exchange for users providing their mobile phone numbers and sharing the malicious link through WhatsApp."
