# LLMs for scam detection (search extract; arxiv.org blocked, abstracts via search)

Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17353 , https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.17353 , https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.16656 ,
https://arxiv.org/html/2511.01746v1 , https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13893v1

- arXiv:2607.17353 (submitted July 19, 2026; Northeastern, Ariel University, Gen Digital): nine LLMs vs a fine-tuned BERT classifier; bigger models usually better; effective prompting noticeably improved smaller ones; LLMs handled unfamiliar scam types better than fine-tuned models; released a real-world scam benchmark.
- Search summary of the same paper: "ChatGPT 4o1 and Llama 3.1 70B reaching 64% and 65% micro-F1/precision/recall, respectively."
- "Read This Paper to Get $50 Million: An Analysis of Mobile Messaging Scams Using Reddit Data" (arXiv 2605.16656): "LLM precision is insufficient for real-world deployment in mobile messaging contexts"; "well-structured, link-centric scams are often detected reliably, whereas conversational, reply-driven scams remain challenging."
- Scam Shield (arXiv 2511.01746): multi-model voting + fine-tuned LLMs; LLMs "remain susceptible to adversarial attacks, such as subtle linguistic modifications by synonym substitution."
