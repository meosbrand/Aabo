## ScamShield bot (Singapore Police Force / Open Government Products)
- Intake: "To check whether a message is a scam, send the scammer's message or contact info to the Bot" by pasting text, sending a screenshot, or forwarding it from another WhatsApp chat.
- Method: "The ScamShield Bot identifies messages based on whether similar messages, numbers, and URLs have been previously reviewed and blacklisted by SPF. We also gather information from the public and check how many reports there are for similar messages. Lastly, the Bot uses a machine learning classifier."
- Status conflict: the bot endpoint states "this WhatsApp bot is no longer in service. You can still check and report scams easily with the ScamShield app."; another ScamShield page still presents the bot as available (undated).

## CheckMate (volunteer-run, better.sg)
- "CheckMate allows anyone to forward messages, images or screenshots that require clarification to a WhatsApp number."
- "behind the scenes, volunteer checkers assess what is sent, and vote on it promptly to issue a quick response."
- "CheckMate relies on crowdsourcing fact-checking by multiple human checkers which diminish bias and strengthens the credibility of fact-checking processes."
- "CheckMate leverages machine learning techniques like natural language processing and generative AI to respond to users' messages quickly."
- Scope: "climate, health and CPF-related misinformation, scams, and illicit messages."
- Newer event listing: pairs "volunteer-based crowdsourcing and agentic AI" so people can check content on demand.
