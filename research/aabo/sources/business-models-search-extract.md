## Huntress
- "Instead of targeting SMBs directly, they went after the managed service providers (MSPs) that serve them."
- CEO: "We've got about 4000 of those. And they bring us to about 110,000 of those SMBs".
- Africa entry through distributor QBS Software Africa: "The initial rollout will focus on South Africa before moving into additional Sub-Saharan African markets."; channel development "still depends heavily on regional knowledge, existing commercial relationships and support."
- Founders funded early operations from a services business, then "sought to build a scalable product company".
- "consistent annual revenue growth exceeding 70% over the past two years ... expected to reach roughly $100M in annual recurring revenue (ARR) in 2024."

## Hoxhunt
- "gives all employees basic security training rather than marketing solely to security teams, and focuses on rewarding success rather than punishing failures."
- AI makes simulations "personalized, adaptive, and dynamic"; "gamified experience"; raised $40M; built for enterprises.

## CybSafe
- Series B $28M, total $40M (2022); clients incl. Barclays, HSBC, Credit Suisse; "strategic partnership with telecoms giant, Vodafone."
- Revenue estimates (third party): $7.7M (2023), $11.5M (2024, estimated). Founding year conflicts (2015 vs 2017).

## Cyberhaven (contrast)
