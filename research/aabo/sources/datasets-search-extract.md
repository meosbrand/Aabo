# Datasets (search extract)

Sources: https://github.com/AbayomiAlli/SMS-Spam-Dataset , https://preview.aclanthology.org/setup/2025.africanlp-1.23 ,
https://github.com/ipridem/smishguard , https://arxiv.org/html/2402.18430v2 , https://huggingface.co/datasets/arkamath2026/sms_spam

- Nigeria (FUNAAB, Abeokuta) SMS-Spam-Dataset: local collection "reflecting African English usage"; 5,240 texts — 2,350 spam, 2,890 ham; general spam, not smishing-specific.
- MOZ-Smishing (AfricaNLP 2025): crowd-sourced Mozambican mobile-money SMS, ham vs smishing, Portuguese; notes public mobile-money smishing data is scarce; tests few-shot LLM detection.
- SmishGuard (Zimbabwe): synthetic legitimate + six fraud types incl. Shona/English and Ndebele/English.
- Smishtank (arXiv 2402.18430): 1,062 community-submitted smishing messages with sender, brand, domain details, VirusTotal results.
- SMS Spam Collection v1: 5,574 English messages (ham/spam).
