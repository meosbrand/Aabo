## Whoscall (Gogolook)
- Can "identify, detect, and filter scam and spam messages, calls, and URLs" (Scam Watch Pilipinas co-founder).
- "Message URL Scanner" checks links in texts; Web Checker "works with Gogolook, the Criminal Investigation Bureau database, and URL risk detection service provider Scamadviser's global database."
- Community: "Report numbers or messages and help protect the community against scams".
- Premium: automatic scanning of received messages, offline database extension, automatic database updates ("Not all regions").
- "ID Security" leak check by phone number; auto-warning when a scam link is opened.
- 2023: "more than 2.6 billion entries to detect unknown callers, spam and scam calls and texts, and potentially harmful URLs".

## Norton Genie
- Upload a screenshot or paste text; Genie "will extract text, scan for links, and use a bit of machine learning"; allows follow-up questions.
- PCWorld got a "Not sure" verdict on an obvious scam text.
- Techlicious: effective on fake delivery notifications, gift-card offers, account-access notices, hiring messages; "more nuanced social engineering tactics, such as the common 'Are you free for coffee' scams, slipped through."
- Pickr 2023: roughly four in five tests successful, screenshots doing well.

## Trend Micro ScamCheck / Scam Radar
- "ScamCheck analyses content in a variety of forms, such as an image or screenshot, a text or email message, a URL link, or a phone number".
- April 2025 (ScamCheck 2.0): on Android, scam link detection works in messaging apps like LINE and Facebook Messenger.
- June 2025: Scam Radar analyses "different communications methods used by scammers in a coordinated fashion such as SMS, websites, and messaging apps", with real-time early alerts.
